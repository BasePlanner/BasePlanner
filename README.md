Wardogs Base Planner
Plan your Wardogs base in 3D. Draw. Build. Defend. 
What it does
Place your F.O.B. first, then build inside its zone. Add more F.O.B.s where the zones do not overlap.
Stack H-blocks, tall H-blocks, Hesco walls, doors and buildings, within the build limit.
Edit the blocks and sandbags inside bunkers, shelters and recon towers in 3D.
Supplies tracker with a breakdown of what the logistics team has to bring (pallets, large crates, small crates, loose supplies).
Save blueprints in your browser, and share a base with a short 5-character code.
Three themes, and touch controls for phones.
